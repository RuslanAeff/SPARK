Template prepared 2026-09-19; not a record of work performed. Create a completed copy for one actual project session. Use the project's established documentation if it already covers these fields. Write research records in English and explanations to the student in Turkish.

## Candidate episodes — not final sample selection

For each genuinely located candidate record:

- Candidate ID, title and one bounded problem/feature.
- Start/end or observation cutoff, and why those boundaries are justified.
- Sources for the initial requirement/prompt; `UNKNOWN` where absent.
- Sources for the specific AI response/patch; separate AI involvement in a correction from authorship of the original defect.
- Human decision/intervention and its source; distinguish the student's recollection from contemporaneous evidence.
- Observed attempts/revisions; do not equate commit count with AI iteration count.
- Before/after implementation references.
- Automated check: command, date, environment/version, actual outcome, log reference and limitations; `NOT_RUN` if not run.
- Runtime/device observation, build/scenario/date/outcome, or `NOT_OBSERVED`.
- Acceptance: who accepted exactly what and where recorded, or `NOT_RECORDED`.
- Unresolved alternatives, evidence gaps and source conflicts.
- Proposed comparative relevance to the thesis; reasons for retaining/excluding, without forcing a quota.
- Provisional labels with reasons and method version, if useful; do not claim formal coding under an unapproved protocol.

E0 narrative/reconstruction, E1 implementation, E2 automated verification, E3 identified runtime/device observation, E4 scoped human acceptance/release: attach the types to individual claims. They are not a global quality score. Missing device evidence remains missing after an automated test passes.
